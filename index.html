A starting point for closer reading

A finding points to a specific part of a paper that may need attention: a claim, proof, assumption, calculation, or experiment. The aim is to help readers understand the concern and check it against the published work.

Follow the evidence

Each audit brings the claim, its location, and the recorded reasoning together. Assessments of what an issue could affect come from the GPT audit and should be considered alongside its evidence.

Keep the judgment human

AI findings can be mistaken or incomplete. These reports support careful reading; readers decide whether an argument is convincing. The site does not give papers an overall correctness rating.

How to read an audit

  1. Choose a volume and paper

    Open JMLR and use the volume selector. Search within a volume by paper title or a word from a finding. Volume and paper links can be copied and shared.

  2. Get the context, then the concern

    The authors’ abstract describes the paper in its authors’ words. The audit finding below it is an editorial summary of the concern recorded by the GPT audit.

  3. Follow the argument

    Select View audit or the paper title. The reading guide explains the claim, what may be wrong, the evidence, and why it may matter. The full GPT audit report follows with the detailed technical argument.

  4. Check the original

    Use Original paper or Open PDF to compare the finding with the published text. Paper locations and source links help you find the relevant passage. Mathematical expressions are typeset; wide equations can be scrolled on a small screen.

What the labels mean

Review incomplete
The GPT audit recorded a finding, but did not finish its overall review of that paper. The finding remains available with this note.
No findings published yet
No findings are published for that volume in this snapshot. This does not establish that every paper in the volume has been reviewed or is free of issues.
Supporting files
The reports link to available public sources. Some audit-generated supporting files are not part of this release; their availability is stated beside the finding.
